## 📌 Incident Report: Malicious Infringement and Fake Tutorials by "94CTO"

**Published**: September 17, 2026
**Infringing Entity**: 94CTO Resource Site (cpp.code.94cto.com)
**Infringing Link (Removed)**: https://cpp.code.94cto.com/index/index/content/id/170513

### 🔍 Statement of Offenses

In September 2026, we received reports from community developers regarding an unauthorized website that scraped our official documentation for the Chronos Seal project and used generative AI to maliciously alter and fabricate completely false usage tutorials.

Their specific egregious actions include:

1. **Fabricated Configuration Logic**: Fabricating `.env` files and local compilation steps such as `npm run build:fork` in their so-called tutorials to mislead users.
2. **Severe Security Misguidance**: If developers followed these instructions, it would lead to the plaintext leakage of the game's core key (Seed), completely destroying the "Zero-Knowledge" security defense designed to protect creators, and causing severe asset security risks to independent game authors.
3. **Fraudulent Credit Scams**: This fake tutorial was priced at "1 credit" for download, which is a malicious act of exploiting the information gap to deceive community users.

### ⚖️ Enforcement Process and Outcome

In response to this egregious plagiarism and fraud, we decisively took legal and enforcement actions:

- **Evidence Preservation**: We completed a full electronic notarization of this project on BluSea (Beijing Guoxin Notary Public Office), securing all evidence of the infringing content.
- **Formal Enforcement**: We submitted a formal infringement complaint ticket with notarization and a DMCA notice to the platform's customer service.
- **Outcome**: Under the deterrence of the law, the platform's customer service has fully removed the relevant infringing pages (the original link is now 404 and inaccessible).

### 📜 Disclaimer and Anti-Counterfeiting Statement

All official documentation, tutorials, and release packages for Chronos Seal are published exclusively on the following platforms:

- **GitHub**: github.com/CrCLARE
- **Official Docs**: docs.crclare.top
- **Official Website**: chronos.crclare.top (Coming Soon)
- **Official QQ Group**: 1062813220

Any third-party websites outside of the above channels claiming "one-click compilation," "internal tools," or similar terms are illegally scraped or AI-fabricated. Developers are strongly advised to use only official channels to prevent financial loss and key leakage.

CrCLARE Studio welcomes all developers to use or create derivative works based on Chronos Seal, but please comply with the NOTICE terms.
